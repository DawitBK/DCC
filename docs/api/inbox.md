# Unified inbox

The inbox is read and written through the operational engine. DCC does not store a second conversation thread table.

## Endpoints

| Method | Path                                    | Permission               | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------ | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GET    | `/api/inbox`                            | `inbox.read`             | Lists messages visible through the caller's account scope. Supports the query fields in `InboxQuery`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| POST   | `/api/inbox/reply`                      | `inbox.reply`            | Sends one reply through the governed engine. Returns `201` with `{ "sent": true }`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| PUT    | `/api/inbox/assignment`                 | `inbox.reply`            | Body `{socialAccountId, engineMessageId, assigneeUserId}`; `assigneeUserId: null` clears the assignment. Returns `{socialAccountId, engineMessageId, assignedTo, assignedToName, assignedAt}`. 400 if the assignee does not hold `inbox.reply` for the company (another company's person, someone who only reads, an unknown id); 404 for an account outside the caller's companies or a message that is not on that account; 403 without `inbox.reply` for the company; 409 for an inactive company or archived account. A change is audited (`inbox.assigned`, `inbox.unassigned`); the same assignee again changes and audits nothing. |
| GET    | `/api/inbox/assignees?companyId=<uuid>` | `inbox.reply`            | The people a message of that company can be assigned to (`[{userId, displayName}]`): everyone holding `inbox.reply` for it. 404 for a company the caller cannot reply for.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| GET    | `/api/inbox/settings`                   | `inbox.read`             | `[{companyId, responseTargetMinutes, updatedAt}]` for each company the caller may read the inbox of. `responseTargetMinutes` is `null` when the company has no target.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| PUT    | `/api/inbox/settings`                   | `approval_policy.manage` | Body `{companyId, responseTargetMinutes}`: whole minutes from 1 to 43200, or `null` to clear. Audited as `inbox.response_target_set` with the previous and new value.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## Assignment and response times (H-14)

DCC still stores no message text. It keeps, per message that has been assigned or answered through DCC, one row (`inbox_items`): the
assignee (who, by whom, when) and the time of the first reply sent through DCC. The message time is copied when the row is made.
Each company has an optional target (`inbox_settings`, `NULL` = none). **There is no default target**: the numbers come from the
marketing lead (A-07), so until a company sets one nothing is ever overdue.

`GET /api/inbox` adds these fields to each message (always sent; optional in the TypeScript type only so older code compiles):

| Field                                        | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `assignedTo`, `assignedToName`, `assignedAt` | the assignee's user id and display name and when they were assigned; `null` when nobody                                                                                                  |
| `firstResponseAt`                            | when the first reply went out **through DCC**; `null` otherwise                                                                                                                          |
| `responseSeconds`                            | whole seconds from receipt to `firstResponseAt` (never negative); `null` when there is no recorded reply                                                                                 |
| `dueAt`                                      | `receivedAt` + the target; `null` without a target                                                                                                                                       |
| `overdue`                                    | `true` only with a target, and when it was missed: a recorded reply that took longer than the target, or no reply and the due time has passed (exactly on the due time is still in time) |

A message the engine reports as `replied` but that has no reply of ours (answered directly in the engine) has `replied: true`,
`firstResponseAt: null` and is **not** called overdue: DCC cannot know how long it took and does not guess. A later reply never moves
`firstResponseAt`. Changing the target changes `dueAt` and `overdue` for existing messages at once (nothing is frozen at receipt).

New query filters on `GET /api/inbox`: `assignedTo=<user id>`, `unassigned=true|false`, `overdue=true|false`.

Who may do what: anyone with `inbox.reply` for the company may assign, reassign and clear (the people who answer are the people who
distribute the work); the response target is managed with `approval_policy.manage` (company administrator, group administrator), the
same gate as the company's other rules, so no new permission was added to the role table. A dedicated `inbox.manage` can replace it
later without changing this contract. Assigning a message **notifies the assignee in the app** (`inbox.assigned`, migration `202611241200`; `targetType` `inbox_item`, `targetId` the `inbox_items` id, summary "A message on <account name> was assigned to you."; it carries no message text or author). Never when the assignee is the person who assigned, never on a repeat of the same assignment, never on clearing. The notification is created in the same transaction as the assignment and is personal (only the assignee reads it). The assignee also finds the message with `assignedTo=<own id>`.

Proven only against the fake engine and the practice engine; the real inbox follows W-08.
