# Company onboarding

How a subsidiary joins DCC. All five team members take part (proposal 6 of the team document). Abenezer owns this folder; anything sensitive stays out of the repository (data rule in OWNERSHIP).

## Checklist (per company)

Copy into the private tracker for each company and tick as done:

1. [ ] Company and organisational structure confirmed (Abenezer)
2. [ ] Brands and social accounts registered in DCC; account inventory complete with no passwords (Abenezer, Hirko)
3. [ ] Account owners and responsible employees identified; **backup administrator and corporate recovery contacts** named for every account (Abenezer)
4. [ ] Administrators and users created, roles assigned (company administrator in DCC; people sign in through SSO) (Hirko)
5. [ ] Approval workflow configured from the Group template and agreed with the company (Hirko, Abenezer)
6. [ ] Accounts connected through each network's official authorisation and validated with a test post (Woluman)
7. [ ] Responsible teams trained (Dawit, Rahel demonstrates)
8. [ ] User acceptance test passed (Abenezer validates against the requirements)
9. [ ] Readiness for production confirmed and signed (Dawit)

Do not connect a real account before the security review of the release in use (assumption ASM-07).

The same steps with the proof for each one, and what to do after go-live and when someone leaves: [company onboarding checklist with evidence](../rollout/COMPANY-ONBOARDING-CHECKLIST.md).
