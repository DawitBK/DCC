## Shared company and product description

> Kerchanshe Group is an Ethiopian business group. DCC (Digital Communications Control Center) is an internal tool for the Group's official social media accounts. Employees prepare content in DCC, an authorized reviewer approves the exact version for the relevant company and account, and DCC records the decision and publication outcome. Postiz is a separate publishing service used by DCC after approval. DCC is not offered to customers or the public.

Confirm the Group's approved legal description, the final list of participating subsidiaries and the privacy wording with Dawit before reuse. Do not add claims about live features: the real-network adapter, media upload and inbox have not all been proven.

### Verified review prerequisites

- **DCC's public review surface is not ready to promise:** Meta requires reviewers to access and exercise each feature under review; LinkedIn Standard tier asks for a screen recording and test access. DCC is currently an internal app, and its publishing and inbox capabilities are not all proven against real networks. Do not submit an app-review request until Dawit approves an externally reachable review plan and the requested features work.
- **Privacy policy and public website are dependencies:** Google requires a verified domain and privacy policy for an external production OAuth app. Ask Dawit to name the public site/domain owner and arrange a DCC privacy policy that accurately describes the data flows before requesting OAuth verification.
- **Ask Abenezer for the account inventory (A-02):** the exact accounts, networks, account owners and priority order determine which products and permissions to request first. Do not request every possible permission up front.
