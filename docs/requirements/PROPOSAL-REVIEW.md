## External dependencies

Network developer-app approvals and business verification (Meta, LinkedIn Community Management API, Google/YouTube quota, TikTok app review and Content Posting audit, X access and cost);
the SSO owner's answers; a host for Postiz; legal view on AGPL and data protection; real organisational data from the subsidiaries.

## Security and compliance risks to watch

Approval bypass through Postiz; public repository discipline; account credentials circulating in spreadsheets today; weak audit assurance without off-box checkpoints; unclear data-protection basis.
See [RISK-REGISTER](../project/RISK-REGISTER.md) and [THREAT-MODEL](../security/THREAT-MODEL.md).
